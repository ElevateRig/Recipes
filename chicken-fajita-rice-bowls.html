Chicken Fajita Rice Bowls

Yield: 3 servings

Ingredients

  • 542 g chicken breast
  • 270 g dry Dynasty jasmine rice
  • 340 g Sprouts vegetable fajita blend
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 12 g Kinder's The Taco Blend seasoning
  • Juice of 1/2 lime
  • Black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Cook the jasmine rice according to package directions.
  2. Cut chicken into bite-size pieces and season with Kinder's Taco Blend and lime juice.
  3. Cook chicken until browned and 165°F internally.
  4. Cook fajita vegetables until tender and lightly browned.
  5. Combine chicken and vegetables. The cooked chicken/vegetable mixture weighed 586 g. Divide into 3 portions of about 195 g each and serve over equal portions of rice.
