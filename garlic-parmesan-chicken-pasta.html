Garlic Parmesan Chicken Pasta

Yield: 3 servings   |   Finished batch: 1,333 g

Ingredients

  • 558 g chicken breast cutlets
  • 228 g dry Bionaturae sourdough fusilli pasta
  • 340 g California blend vegetables
  • 56 g American Grana Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1/2 to 1 cup reserved pasta water

Directions

  1. Cook pasta al dente. Reserve pasta water before draining.
  2. Season chicken and cook until browned and 165°F internally.
  3. Cook vegetables; add minced garlic for the final 30–60 seconds.
  4. Return chicken and pasta to the skillet over low heat. Add oil and a little pasta water, then gradually toss in Parmesan until creamy.
  5. Divide into 3 servings. Finished batch weight was 1,333 g.
